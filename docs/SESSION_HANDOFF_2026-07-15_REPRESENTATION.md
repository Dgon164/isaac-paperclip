## ATTACK LIST FOR THIS SEGMENT (probe BEFORE trusting — the house
## law; these are the ways it fools itself)
- SELECTION ACROSS LENSES: N lenses = N chances to hallucinate —
  the 119/120 dialogue lesson; every agreement claim needs the
  scrambled/max-null selection correction.
- MINT-ONLY-WORKS-THROUGH-THE-RESERVOIR: a minted node must prove
  itself with the reservoirs REMOVED (regeneration, discrimination
  on fresh data, ordinary nulls) — else the lens became the answer
  (forbidden: no-nets-as-deliverable).
- NOISE WORLDS MINT NOTHING: genesis on pure noise and on scrambled
  data must produce zero mints at the stated false-alarm rate.
- TAIL-CHASING: genesis must not mint features of its OWN artifacts
  (e.g., binning edges, floor quantization) — plant known artifacts
  and verify they are refused or attributed to the instrument.
- DRIFT: the 13-inch foot must be CONSISTENTLY 13 — a lens/ruler
  that changes between readings poisons relationships; consistency
  measured per instrument before its readings count.
- COST ACCOUNTING: ensembles are cheap (~24ms/verdict measured) but
  not free at scale — complete numbers, no silent caps.
- THE KINDS RUNG: v1 mints combinations/axes/groups/relations of
  EXISTING structural kinds; inventing new KINDS is named research,
  not claimed capability — do not let a demo overclaim it.
