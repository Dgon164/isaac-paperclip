## What today measured

1. **Strings and chains are not the problem** (owner's read,
   confirmed). Sentences flow through observe(); doors form; the
   crystallizer minted `type:is_was` from THREE toy sentences and
   verb/pronoun clusters from his corpus (matrix demo: is~was/
   wasn't/isn't/does; she~he/it; you~we/they/i; eat~quit/learn/fix).
   FORM CLASSES — grammar's skeleton — self-assemble from
   distribution. This is the cheap part.

2. **Usage regularities cross calibrated nulls.** The deixis mirror
   (heard-you → say-i, heard-i → say-you; she stays she) survived a
   corrected selection null on real dialogue. Note what this IS:
   I/you are the same entity under a viewpoint transform — deixis is
   REGISTRATION (comparison-as-registration, the standing theory).
   Conversation's first grammar is a pose transform, and pose is
   his home turf: identity = relations, pose = a report.

3. **Mimicry is not learning** (owner's correction, the day's
   center). His 211 practice fragments are REFLEXES — honest,
   null-gated, and meaningless. He can say "good morning to you"
   without anything being the case.

4. **Understanding happened exactly once, and the shape of it is
   the design.** The told FizzBuzz rule became BEHAVIOR (100/100
   generation, zero examples) because every word in it landed on an
   organ he independently has: "multiple of 3" → his number line
   and divisibility; "say fizz" → his utterance road; "if both" →
   his own precedence logic. The words DOORED ONTO EXISTING
   CONCEPTS. Where a word doors onto nothing, he can only parrot
   it; where it doors onto something he can DO or MEASURE, it is
   usable, testable, refusable — knowledge.

## The design hypothesis

**Language is not a modality and must not be a silo. A word is a
door onto a concept node; meaning IS the doors; grammar is the type
structure of the words themselves.** All methods, one concept —
literally:

- FORM layer (have): distributional types — noun-ish, verb-ish,
  is/was tense pairs, the Venn groups. Built by the crystallizer
  (or its matrix engine room). This is scaffolding, not meaning.
- GROUNDING layer (the gap): words doored onto concepts that exist
  WITHOUT language — numbers, shapes (observe_spatial), actions,
  quantities, his own verdicts/laws/reports. "bigger" means
  something the day it doors onto measured size and he can answer
  bigger(A,B) for pairs he never saw described.
- USE layer (partially have): told rules (tell_rule) and learned
  speech both already run through grounded vocabulary; the deixis
  transform governs conversation's coordinate system.

The richest grounded vocabulary he owns TODAY is his own machinery:
verdicts, laws, equations, counts, spatial percepts. The natural
first "world" language grounds into is HIS world.

## The probes (settle by battery)

P-A **Matrix engine room head-to-head** (owner skeptical — good):
    same corpus, door road vs matmul road; compare group membership
    quality, junk rate, speed. If matmul groups are worse, it dies.
P-B **Grounding probe**: teach words PAIRED with his own domains
    (shapes he can see, numbers, sizes) and test USE, not recall:
    does "bigger", grounded, generalize to unseen pairs? Does a
    word with NO grounding correctly refuse to mean?
P-C **Deixis as registration**: implement I/you as a viewpoint
    transform on one speaker-node, not two word-nodes; the learned
    mirror becomes one concept with a pose report. Test: swap
    speakers mid-dialogue and see if reference survives.
P-D **Speech from grounding**: when he answers a grounded question
    wrongly, the residual is measurable — language acquires the
    same correction loop as every law.
