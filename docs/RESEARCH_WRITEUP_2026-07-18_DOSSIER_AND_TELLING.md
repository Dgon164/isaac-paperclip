## PART 3 — THE TELLING ROAD ("ohm's law is v=ir")

— it owns no knowledge and mints no new node kinds. Knowledge

application fact-bundle (uses-form / output / slots taught as
facts) — so **the name survives the shared shape**: ohm and

The v1 grammar, declared (ceilings named, never silent):
`<name> is <out>=<expr>`; single-letter quantities (physics style
— `ir` reads i·r); `pi`; powers including halves and negatives;
squares emitted as the house canonical `x*x`; division; NO
parentheses or functions (refused by name); `/ab` refused as
ambiguous. **READ PROPOSES, THE ECHO CONFIRMS**: every accepted
telling is echoed back rendered from what was actually stored.
